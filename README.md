PCB & PCBA Manufacturing – Core Driver and Directional CPAK Assembly

Technical documentation and manufacturing process support for PCB products and PCBA assemblies, including Core Driver and Directional CPAK products for Baker Hughes.

Technical Responsibilities

- PCB Product Documentation: Product file creation, assembly documentation, revision tracking, and manufacturing data preparation.
- BOM Review: Component part number verification, package identification, value checks, and BOM-to-assembly data cross-checking.
- Gerber Analysis: Review of PCB fabrication layers, copper tracks, solder mask, silkscreen, drill files, and board layout data.
- SMT Assembly: Surface-mount component placement and stuffing at top and bottom PCB stages, including component orientation and polarity verification.
- Reflow Soldering: Review and verification of reflow oven temperature profiles, preheat, soak, peak temperature, time above liquidus (TAL), and cooling parameters according to component and solder-paste specifications.
- Through-Hole Assembly (THT): Component insertion, lead forming, orientation checks, and solder joint inspection.
- Selective Soldering: Process support for localized soldering of through-hole components, including temperature, contact time, and soldering parameter verification.
- Tinning and Lead Forming: Component lead preparation, tinning, and forming according to assembly requirements.
- Press-Fit Assembly: Support for press-fit component insertion and verification of alignment and assembly requirements.
- Gluing and Adhesive Application: Component bonding and adhesive application according to manufacturing specifications.
- Quality Assurance: Preparation of stage-wise QA checklists, inspection criteria, assembly verification, and process documentation.
- Manufacturing Process Control: Verification of process settings, assembly instructions, workmanship, and product traceability.
